# IdeaForge - Product Requirements Document (PRD)

# 1. Problem Statement

Students, founders, and hackathon participants often validate ideas without critically analyzing them. Many rely on AI-generated suggestions that sound convincing but are not systematically challenged or evaluated.

There is currently no system that:

* Generates alternative ideas
* Critiques ideas from multiple perspectives
* Evolves ideas based on feedback
* Compares competing ideas
* Produces an execution-ready recommendation

IdeaForge aims to provide structured idea evaluation rather than simple idea generation.

---

# 2. Product Vision

IdeaForge is an AI-powered Startup Survival Simulator.

Users provide a startup domain, problem statement, or hackathon track.

The system generates multiple candidate ideas, evaluates them through AI judges, evolves weak ideas, compares competing ideas, and produces the strongest surviving idea with an execution roadmap.

---

# 4. User Journey

User enters a startup problem.

↓

System generates multiple startup ideas.

↓

Ideas are evaluated and criticized.

↓

Weak ideas are improved.

↓

Ideas compete through tournament rounds.

↓

Best surviving idea is selected.

↓

Execution roadmap is generated.

---

# 7. Non Goals

IdeaForge is NOT:

* A startup idea generator only
* A business plan generator
* A pitch deck generator
* A chatbot
* A guaranteed startup success predictor

---

# 8. Trust Model

IdeaForge does not claim to predict startup success.

Instead, it improves decision-making through:

* Multiple generators
* Multiple critics
* Transparent scoring
* Structured evaluation
* Idea evolution history

Goal:

Provide better decision support than a single AI response.

---

# 9. Final Report
